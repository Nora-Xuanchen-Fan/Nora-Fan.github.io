01 Profile

Researcher, strategist and attentive observer.

I am a social researcher with interests spanning gender, the body, social inequality, cultural policy and creative industries.

Through the Executive Master of Arts at the University of Melbourne, I have developed an interdisciplinary practice connecting qualitative inquiry, policy analysis, cultural strategy and clear public-facing communication.

My work asks how institutions, public narratives and everyday decisions shape whose experiences are seen—and whose needs are acted upon.
