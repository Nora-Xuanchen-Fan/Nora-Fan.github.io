02 About

Ideas become useful when they stay close to lived experience.

I am a social researcher with interests spanning gender, the body, social inequality, cultural policy and creative industries. My work asks how institutions, public narratives and everyday decisions shape whose experiences are seen—and whose needs are acted upon.

Through the Executive Master of Arts at the University of Melbourne, I have developed an interdisciplinary practice connecting qualitative inquiry, policy analysis, cultural strategy and clear public-facing communication.
